Algorithm

  1. Commitment: before the bet the game shows SHA256(serverSeed) as lowercase hex. Every round (demo rounds included, any number of cards) uses the next nonce of the seed pair, starting from 1.
  2. 20 balls are drawn without replacement from 1 … 51. For ball i = 0 … 19: h = HMAC-SHA256(key = serverSeed + ":" + i, message = clientSeed + ":" + nonce), as 64 lowercase hex digits.
  3. start = int(h[0 … 15), 16) mod 47; num = int(h[start … start + 14), 16) >> 3 (a 53-bit integer).
  4. index = floor(num × left / 253), where left = 51 − i is the count of numbers not drawn yet; the ball is the number at that index of the remaining numbers in ascending order.
  5. A card is 5 × 5 with 24 different numbers from 1 … 51 and a free centre. There are 12 lines: 5 rows, 5 columns and 2 diagonals; a line wins when all 5 of its cells are drawn (the centre always counts).
  6. Multiplier by winning lines on a card: 1 → 5×, 2 → 50×, 3 → 260×, 4 → 520×, 5 → 1000×, 6 or more → 10000×, none → 0. Card payout = amount per card × multiplier, truncated to 4 decimals; the round pays the sum over all cards (RTP ≈ 98.01%).
  7. This page floors the exact product. Some other verifiers convert the floating-point product with parseInt, which differs only when the product is below 10−6 (about 2 × 10−8 per ball).
  8. Nonces used by rounds that were rejected by the platform or never confirmed have no settled result, so the settled history of a seed may skip some nonces.
